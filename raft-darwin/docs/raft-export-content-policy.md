# Final RAFT export content policy

The user has authorized one cumulative export after all research and primary adjudication through 24 November 1859 are complete. The export must contain the confirmed conversation letter pairs and the unpaired Charles Darwin grounding letters, with appropriate date cutoffs. Every entry must be checked for unwanted apparatus and transcription artifacts. This document records requirements; it is not a converter or an export.

## Content and provenance

Only the attributed letter text belongs in the question, answer or grounding content. Exclude letter headings, addresses and datelines used as headers, editorial summaries, footnotes, annotations, bibliography, navigation, stamps and other excluded apparatus. Preserve participants, date and source URL in structured metadata instead. Keep supporting historical evidence and source hashes in the research records and export audit sidecars, outside the RAFT content.

Do not invent missing prompts or fill missing passages. A relationship can be historically established without all of its text being eligible for export. Material surviving only in a footnote or annotation requires a separate content-eligibility review; the historical admission alone does not authorize copying that layer into RAFT. This applies to the recovered Parker circular question as well as quoted notebooks and annotations.

Preserve original meaning, historical spelling, uncertainty and qualified claims. Smooth extraction and transcription artifacts only where preserved markup or an authoritative transcript or manuscript supports the repair. Examples include joined words, stray markup, rendered fractions and table associations. Do not silently modernize the author's prose or turn an uncertain reading into a certain one. Document each substantive repair with original text, corrected text, source location, evidence and reason. An unresolved artifact holds the affected entry until resolved; account for the hold explicitly.

## Dates and conversation units

Keep composition, posting, receipt, reading and demonstrated knowledge dates distinct. Use one established Darwin response or safely dated response section per transcript, so later turns cannot widen retrieval for earlier replies. An incoming date, receipt date, knowledge date or conservative latest bound cannot automatically replace the response's date. The verified RAFT implementation retrieves documents strictly earlier than the transcript date; unresolved dates must therefore receive explicit treatment before inclusion.

Use only eligible Darwin prose as grounding content. Incoming letters belong on the question side of supported exchanges. Deduplicate shared response bodies and preserve scoped relationships; several incoming links do not necessarily imply several independent training examples.

## Per-entry and cumulative checks

For every entry, verify source identity, authorship, accepted relationship and text scope, date policy, schema, and absence of headers, footnotes, annotations and unresolved artifacts. Check the final rendered text against the preserved source, not merely against a cleaning function's output. Record the check and source hashes outside the training text.

Before final delivery, reconcile all periods and additive corrections; account for every eligible pair and unpaired Darwin body, every exclusion and every unresolved hold. Validate gapless one-based transcript numbering, two-string exchanges, corpus separation, duplicate handling and date filtering. Export authorization does not authorize embeddings, training or paid calls.
