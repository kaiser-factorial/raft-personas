- **448:** XML encodes only 30 November, whereas the displayed date and body continue on 1 December. The new graph has no whole-letter scalar date and distinguishes Friday from Saturday. The original XML remains unchanged.
- **481 and 484:** explicit weekday headers fix their first sections; internal datelines fix their continuations. The initial Luna date review missed the headers. Both the initial and corrected reviews remain available.
- **626:** the new Emma letter arrives at paragraph 8 during the same calendar day. Paragraphs 1–7 cannot use its newly arrived information. Its incoming text remains unlocated.
- **565F:** the surviving text is incomplete, and the header preserves an older reading while the editorial note discusses conservation and a presumed correction to 11 May 1840. Preserve all readings and hold an unconditional scalar date pending further source review.
- **704:** retain the 12–24 October 1843 range. A Wednesday header suggests the 18th, but the weighing note has incompatible timing and a different recorded weight; the candidate day is not promoted.
- **716F:** preserve Darwin's invalid “Nov. 31.” alongside the editorially resolved 1 December date. Editorial brackets alone do not make a resolved date unusable.
- **437 and 601:** references to Emma's note to Catherine and Susan's letter to Emma are third-party documents, excluded from the requested to/from-Darwin corpus. They do not become invented incoming prompts.
- **471:** later autobiographical evidence that Darwin kept Emma's theological letter does not establish a contemporary knowledge date for this young-Darwin corpus.

## Review method and preserved evidence

Use scripts for complete candidate inventories, original dates, hashes, coverage and exact-quote checks. Give Lunas bounded correspondent groups to read in full, including negative conclusions and competing candidates. The primary agent then checks every proposed positive relation against full texts, dates, authorship and earlier context, and reviews negative cases for missed matches. An additional independent pass is most useful for multi-day letters, uncertain identities and evidence reused across correspondents. This batch supports that division of work, but it is not a measured cost or accuracy benchmark.

The [parent review](parent_review.json) records coverage, exact quotations checked, source hashes and corrections. The [graph](../correspondence-map/graph.json), [summary](../correspondence-map/summary.json), and [aggregate status](../../corpus-status.json) are generated from the parent ledger. All 56 body-source hashes, 476 XML-source hashes and eight earlier baseline artifacts are checked unchanged. Incoming text always retains its author and never becomes Darwin voice.
